> Inside the cube: *t³ made literal*

The three axes of this cube are T₁, T₂, T₃ — not space, but time in three directions. Sweep the T₃ slice and the grid intersections on the plane flare as it passes. Take ω₃ toward zero and the field flattens along T₃: the fine phase relegating itself to the coupling, 1/137. The view-lock planes are the mixed volumetric products: T₁T₂ is the t·τ plane, and the mixed volumes t²·τ and τ²·t are the slabs swept as the T₃ slice travels through them.
