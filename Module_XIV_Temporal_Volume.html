XIV The temporal volume

Inside the cube: t³ made literal

The three axes of this cube are T₁, T₂, T₃ — not space, but time in three directions. The interior is the cubic-time interference field itself: standing nodes where the three temporal phases agree, voids where they cancel. Drag the cube to turn it, and switch to Cubic mesh to see the geodesic time mesh promoted from its t² surface into full t³ structure — a wireframe that lives in the volume and warps with the field.

Interference-node mode is the product field cosω₁T₁·cosω₂T₂·cosω₃T₃ — the same form the Scramble Zone damps with e−γt. Beat-alignment mode sums the phases, and the bright planes are where the temporal beats agree. In Cubic mesh render, the lattice lines are the mesh field itself: every gridline displaces radially with the local field value — the t² surface's undulation, promoted one dimension — so the volume visibly breathes where the phases constructively align. Sweep the T₃ slice and the grid intersections on the plane flare as it passes. Take ω₃ toward zero and the field flattens along T₃: the fine phase relegating itself to the coupling, 1/137. The view-lock planes are the mixed volumetric products: T₁T₂ is the t·τ plane, and the mixed volumes t²·τ and τ²·t are the slabs swept as the T₃ slice travels through them.
